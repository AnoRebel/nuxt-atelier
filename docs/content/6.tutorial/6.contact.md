Forms are content too: editors build them in the admin, with the fields they need, and place them on pages with the **Form** block. Submissions are stored, listed in the admin, and emailed. The site only styles them.

## Build the form

In the admin, open **Forms** and create "Contact": a **Name** (text, required), an **Email** (email, required) and a **Message** (text area, required). Set the message shown after sending, and under **Notify**, your address. Publish it.

Then create the page "Contact" with a **Page intro** and a **Form** block choosing the form, and add it to the menu in **Settings**.

## Spam

Every form is protected without you doing anything:

- a **honeypot** field people don't see: filled in, the submission is dropped as if it was sent;
- a **minimum fill time**: sent within three seconds of loading the page, it's dropped the same way;
- a **rate limit** per visitor and form (`forms.submissionsPerHour`).

For a challenge as well, set Cloudflare Turnstile's keys, and the form shows its widget:

```bash [.env]
NUXT_ATELIER_FORMS_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY=…
NUXT_ATELIER_FORMS_TURNSTILE_SECRET=…
```

## Email

Submissions are emailed to the form's **Notify** address once the app can send email: give it a provider in `NUXT_ATELIER_MAIL_URL` and a sender address.

```bash [.env]
NUXT_ATELIER_MAIL_URL=resend://re_…          # or postmark://…, or smtp://user:password@host:587 with nodemailer installed
NUXT_ATELIER_MAIL_FROM=Rayleigh <studio@example.com>
```

Emails go out as background jobs, retried when the provider fails. Every submission is also in the admin, under the form's **Submissions**, to read or export as CSV.
