Nuxt Atelier is a Nuxt module. You install it into an app you already have.

## What it adds

- a content schema you define in TypeScript
- storage for that content (SQLite by default, Postgres or MySQL in production)
- a visual, block-based editor and an admin dashboard
- server APIs and composables to render content in your own pages

Try it: the demo below is [Rayleigh](/tutorial/setup), a portfolio built on Nuxt Atelier, and you get a copy of it to change as you like.

::live-demo{path="/admin" label="The admin of a demo site, signed in as its administrator"}
::

## What it leaves alone

It doesn't take over your app: there's no required `app.vue`, layout or root element. Your pages, layouts and components keep working as they are.

## Status

Nuxt Atelier is in early development and not published yet. The [installation](/getting-started/installation) and [quick start](/getting-started/quick-start) pages describe how it works once released.
