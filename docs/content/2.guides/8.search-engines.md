---
title: Search engines and sharing
description: For editors, how a page shows in search results and when shared, and how to improve it.
---

Pages with an address on your site (posts, articles, products…) show a **Search and sharing** panel under their form. It updates as you type.

## The previews

- **In search results**: the address, the title as search engines show it (with your site's name, if your site adds it), and the description.
- **When shared**: the image, title and description a link shows in chat apps and social networks.

## Where the text comes from

If the page has an **SEO** group of fields, what you write there is used. Anything you leave empty there comes from the page itself:

| Shown | Taken from, in order |
|---|---|
| Title | the SEO title, then the page's title or name |
| Description | the SEO description, then its description, excerpt or summary |
| Image | the SEO image, then its image or cover |
| Address | the SEO canonical address, then the page's own |

The SEO group also has **noindex**, which keeps the page out of search results and out of the sitemap, and **nofollow**, which asks search engines not to follow its links.

## The advice

Under the previews, the panel suggests improvements. They're only advice: you can always save.

- **Add a description**: without one, search engines pick some text from the page.
- **Description length**: around 50 to 160 characters fits search results; longer ones are cut.
- **Title length**: over 60 characters, with your site's name, is cut.
- **Image without alt text**: add it in the media library, for people who can't see the image and for search engines.
- **Same title as another entry**: search engines may treat the pages as duplicates; make each title say what's different.

## What search engines may visit

Administrators and roles that manage settings decide what search engines and other crawlers may visit, under **Search engines** in the admin. Turning off **Let search engines index the site** asks every crawler to stay away, for a site that isn't ready yet. Rules can keep particular crawlers (an AI crawler, say) or paths out. The admin itself is always off limits. **Use the configuration** goes back to what your developers set.

Only published pages are in the sitemap search engines read, without the ones marked noindex.
