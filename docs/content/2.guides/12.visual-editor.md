The visual editor shows the real page as visitors will see it, with your unsaved changes, and lets you edit it there. Open an entry and choose **Visual editor**; the button appears on entries that have their own page, when the site has the editor installed.

![The visual editor: the page's structure on the left, the page in the middle with the selected gallery outlined, the gallery's fields on the right](/media/visual-editor.webp)

::live-demo{path="/admin/edit?type=home" label="The visual editor, on the demo's home page"}
::

- **The structure** (left) lists the page's blocks in order, with the blocks inside each one under it, and the blocks you can add.
- **The page** (middle) is the live page with your changes. The selected block is outlined and labeled. Choose a width at the top: desktop, tablet, mobile or your own. A page wider than the space for it is shown scaled down, whole.
- **The side panel** (right) shows the selected block's fields, or the page's comments.

Nothing you do here is live until you save and publish, as with the entry's form. **Back to the form** returns to it, with your changes.

## Selecting and editing

Click a block in the page or in the structure to select it: both show it, and the side panel shows its fields. Change them there and the page follows as you type.

Some text can be edited right on the page: double-click it. Headings and short texts become editable as they are; rich text gets its formatting too: **Ctrl+B** for bold, **Ctrl+I** for italic, `- ` at the start of a line for a list, `## ` for a heading. Click outside it when you're done. If double-clicking does nothing, that text is edited in the side panel.

A block that shows nothing yet, a list of teasers with nothing to list, say, appears as a dashed box with its name, so you can still select, move and delete it. Visitors don't see the box.

Some blocks show one part at a time, like a carousel showing one slide. The editor shows them as a plain box listing everything they contain, so each slide can be selected and moved. The page itself still looks as designed.

## Adding, moving and removing blocks

- **Add** a block by clicking it under **Add to …**: it goes after the selected block, or at the end of the page. Or drag it to where you want it, in the structure or on the page.
- **Move** a block by dragging it in the structure, or by dragging its label on the page. With the keyboard, select it in the structure and press **Alt+↑** or **Alt+↓**; the editor says where it went.
- **Duplicate** and **Delete** are in the side panel. **Delete** works in the structure too.
- **Drop an image** from your computer onto a block that shows one, a picture or a card with an image: it's uploaded to the media library and becomes that block's image. The block is outlined while you drag over it; a block without an image doesn't take the file.

Blocks can only go where they're allowed. A section that takes only cards refuses a headline: while you drag, a line shows where the block would go, and no line means it can't go there. A block the selected spot doesn't take is greyed out under **Add to …**.

## Undo and the command palette

**Undo** and **Redo** (or **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Shift+Z**) step back and forth through your changes since you opened the editor, typing included. They work wherever the focus is, on the page too.

**Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) opens the command palette: type a few letters of what you want and press **Enter**.

![The command palette, filtered to the commands that insert blocks and patterns](/media/visual-editor-commands.webp)

It lists the blocks and patterns you can insert where the selection is, the actions on the selected block, undo, redo and save. Your site may add its own commands.

## Reusing blocks

- **Patterns** and **Shared blocks** are listed under the blocks you can add. A pattern adds copies of its blocks that you can change freely. A shared block is the same everywhere it's used: changing it on one page changes it on all of them (see [Reusing content](/guides/reusing-content)).
- **Reuse this block**, in the side panel, saves the selected block as a pattern, or as a shared block that this page then uses.
- A selected shared block says so in the side panel. **Detach** makes it this page's own copy.
- **Ctrl+C** copies the selected block and **Ctrl+V** pastes it after the selected one, in this entry, another one, or another browser tab. Pasting follows the same rules as adding.

![Reuse this block, opened on a selected gallery: a title, then Save as shared block or Save as pattern](/media/visual-editor-reuse.webp)

## Comments

A block with open comment threads shows a numbered marker on the page. Click it to open the thread in the side panel's **Comments**, where you can reply and resolve as in the entry's form (see [Working together](/guides/working-together)).

![The Comments tab: a comment with Reply, Resolve and Delete, and a box for a new one](/media/visual-editor-comments.webp)
