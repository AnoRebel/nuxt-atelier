## Make a form

In **Forms**, create a form and add its fields. Each field has:

![The contact form being edited: its first field, with a name, type, label and options](/media/form-builder.webp)

- a **name**: how it's stored and exported (letters, digits, `_` and `-`; `email`, `message`…)
- a **type**: text, email, textarea, number, select, checkbox, radio, date, file, hidden or consent
- a **label**, and in **labels** the label in other languages, like `{ "de": "Ihr Name" }`
- whether it's **required**, and its other rules: options for select and radio (one per line, `value` or `value|Label`), a minimum and maximum (numbers, or the length of text), a pattern, accepted file types (`image/*, application/pdf`) and the largest file in MB

Then say what happens after someone sends it:

- **Success**: a message they see, or a page to go to (`/thanks`)
- **Notify**: addresses that get each submission by email, comma-separated
- **Confirmation**: an email to the sender, at the form's first email field
- **Retention**: days submissions are kept (90 by default; 0 keeps them)

A form appears on the site once it's published, and saving checks it: two fields with one name, or a select without options, are refused with the reason.

## Put it on a page

Add the **Form** block to a page and choose the form. The same form can be on many pages.

## What people send

A form's page lists its **Submissions** for those who manage form submissions: search them, filter by date, open uploaded files, export them as **CSV** or **JSON**, or delete one.

![The Submissions section of a form: search and dates, export and erase buttons, and one submission](/media/form-submissions.webp)

Uploaded files are private: they're never on a public address, only here.

**Erase by email** deletes everything someone sent, in every form, files included: for when a person asks you to remove their data. It can't be undone.

Use a **consent** field to ask for permission ("You may store my message"); when it's required, the form can't be sent without it.
