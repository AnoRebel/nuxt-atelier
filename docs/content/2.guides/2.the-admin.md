---
title: The admin
description: For editors, a tour of the admin, where you write and publish your site's content.
---

The admin is at `/admin` on your site (your developers may have put it elsewhere). It works in any recent browser, on a computer or a tablet.

## Signing in

The first time anyone opens the admin, it asks for the first administrator's email and password. After that it asks you to sign in: with your email and password, with a passkey if your site offers them, or with one of the other accounts listed under **Or continue with** (Google, GitHub or your company's sign-in, depending on the site).

## Finding your way

The navigation lists what you may work on:

- **Dashboard**: your unpublished drafts, what's scheduled to go live or come down, and, for administrators, recent activity, background jobs that failed and database updates still to run.
- **Content**: each kind of content your site has: collections of entries (posts, products, authors) and single pages of settings, and **Media**, the library of images and files (see [images and files](/guides/media)).
- **Settings**: users and roles, for those who manage them.
- **Tools**, or groups your site adds: pages your developers built for your site.

You only see what your role allows. On a narrow screen the navigation opens from the menu button at the top.

If your organization runs several sites from one installation, **Site** at the top switches the one you work on, among those you have access to. Lists, media, forms and settings are that site's.

To jump anywhere, press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) or the magnifier at the top: type part of a title, a person's name or a screen's name, move with the arrow keys and press Enter.

## Lists

A collection opens as a list. You can:

- **search** by title;
- **sort** by clicking a column's header (again to reverse it);
- choose the **columns** shown;
- **select** entries with their checkboxes, then publish, unpublish, export (JSON or CSV) or delete them together. If some can't be done, a message says which and why; the rest are done;
- page through long lists at the bottom.

![The list of posts, with their publishing date, status and last change](/media/admin-list.webp)

Each entry shows its status: **Draft** (never published), **Published**, or **Changed** (published, with changes not published yet).

## Editing

Open an entry to edit it, or choose **New**. The form is built from the fields your developers defined; a field your role may only read is shown but can't be changed, and fields you may not see aren't there.

![A post being edited: its title, address, headline, description and cover image](/media/admin-entry.webp)

- **Save draft** keeps your changes without publishing them (see [drafts and publishing](/guides/drafts-and-publishing)). In content without drafts, **Save** publishes straight away.
- When a field needs fixing, the message is under it.
- A media field shows the chosen file: **Choose** opens the library, where you pick a file or upload a new one.
- When a field points to other entries (an author, say) and the one you need doesn't exist yet, choose **New author** under it: you create it in a dialog, it's selected, and nothing you typed in the form is lost.
- If someone else saved the entry after you opened it, saving tells you so instead of overwriting their work: reload to see their changes.

### Formatting text

Rich-text fields have a **Formatting** toolbar: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and code; a heading and a subheading; bulleted and numbered lists, quotes and code blocks; and links. **Link** asks for an address: a web address (`https://…`), an email (`mailto:…`) or a path on this site (`/about`). The usual shortcuts work too (**Ctrl+B**, **Ctrl+I**, **Ctrl+Z**), as does typing `- ` for a list or `## ` for a heading. What's saved is the text and its formatting, never raw HTML, so pasted content keeps only what the site can show.

### Nothing is lost

While you type, the admin keeps a copy of your unsaved changes in your browser. If the browser closes or crashes, opening the entry again offers them back: **Restore them** or **Discard**. Leaving an entry with unsaved changes asks first.

## AI suggestions

If your site has AI assist and your role includes it, text fields offer **Rewrite**, **Shorten**, **Expand** and **Translate to…**, entries can **Suggest a title and description**, and images **Suggest alt text**. A suggestion is only shown to you: edit it, then **Use it** or **Discard**. Using it changes the field, not the saved entry, and **Undo** takes it back.

In the search (**Ctrl+K**), type what you need and choose **Ask the assistant**: it proposes steps, and does nothing until you tick the ones you want and send them. They're done as you, so it can't do anything you couldn't.

## Making the admin yours

The palette button opens **Appearance**: the look of the admin (its material, light or dark, how dense it is, the proportions of your device), whether navigation is a sidebar or a bar at the top, how wide the content is, and the admin's language when your site has several. Your choices stay in your browser. **Alt+Shift+A** opens it from anywhere.

## Signing out

Choose your name at the bottom of the sidebar (or top right with the top bar), then **Sign out**.
