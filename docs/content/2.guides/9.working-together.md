title: Working together
description: For editors, what happens when several people edit the same content, and how to discuss it in comments.
---

## Who's editing

When you open an entry, you hold it for editing: others who open it see that you're editing, and the entry opens **read-only** for them. They can still read it, comment on it and see your saved changes.

You keep the entry while it's open and you're active. If you close the browser, or leave it without typing or clicking for a while (15 minutes by default), the entry is freed a minute later and someone else can edit it.

The avatars at the top of an entry show who else has it open.

### Taking over

If someone left an entry open and you need it now, **Take over** gives it to you. You need the permission to publish that type of content. The person you took it from is told at once, their editor turns read-only, and their unsaved changes stay in their browser: they're offered back the next time they edit the entry.

## Comments

Under each entry, **Comments** holds the discussion about it. Comments aren't content: they're never published and don't appear in the entry's history.

- **On a block**: choose the block under **About** before you write.
- **Mention someone** with **Mention**: they're notified. Only people who can read the entry can be mentioned.
- **Reply** to keep a discussion in one thread, and **Resolve** it when it's settled (or **Reopen** it).

If a block with comments is removed, its comments stay, marked **On a removed block**.

Who may comment is part of your role (the `comment` permission); everyone who can read an entry can read its comments.

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar counts what's new for you: mentions, replies to your comments, and takeovers of your edits. Choose one to open the entry. If your site has email set up, you're emailed too.
